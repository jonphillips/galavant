Mockup from the 2026-11-08 design session. It shows the region guide getting an optional trip lens: when the guide's region falls on a trip's days, a strip of lunch and dinner slots appears above the cards, and the cards can fill those slots. A dossier opens over the map instead of replacing the list. Every place, note, and trip here is invented (the repo is public). Builds on ADR-0053 (guide and dossier), ADR-0035 (alternatives rings), ADR-0012 (per-day regions), and ADR-0029 (meal-aware hours). Nothing on this page needs a schema change except the hotel candidates noted in frame 4.

1 · iPad — Dining, a card tapped, "Add to Trip" open

The list column stays put and the dossier floats over the map's trailing edge. Tapping another card or pin swaps what's in the panel, with no back button. The map stays visible in the middle, and you can still pan it while the panel is open.

  • 1Trip lens. It appears only when this region is assigned to a trip's days (TripDayRegion). With no trip, the guide is exactly what it is today. The chip switches trips if two overlap, and the chevron folds the strip away. On an undated trip, rows read "Day 4" without a weekday.
  • 2Cells show what's already there, wherever it came from: booked (green), a ring with options (amber, ADR-0035), a freeform stop, or an open slot. A lunch or dinner cell is a day's TripIdeas with dayPart .lunch/.dinner, or timed in that window. A travel day is hatched.
  • 3Cards show where they sit in the trip. A badge on a card that's already in a slot keeps you from double-booking it, or tells you it's one of the options for a slot.
  • 4The dossier floats over the map. Tapping another card or pin swaps it, and × closes it. On iPad this replaces IdeaMapSplitView's column swap.
  • 5Meal-aware hours, laid against the trip. The days you're in the region are outlined, using the WeeklyHours data from ADR-0029. It answers "can we even go Thursday?" before you try.
  • 6Add to Trip lists slots, not days. An open slot gets a new stop. A filled one adds this place to that slot's ring (addAlternative). Slots where the place is closed are greyed out with the reason. On iPad you can also drag a card onto a cell (the highlighted Wed lunch). The menu opens beside the panel so the dossier stays readable.

Tapping a filled cell selects that slot. The list narrows to the slot's options, the map shows only them, and you can make one active right there without going back to the trip.

3 · iPhone — strip as day cards; dossier as a sheet over the map

There's no room for a grid on a phone, so the strip becomes a row of day cards you swipe sideways. In list mode a tap still opens the dossier, but as a sheet that rises to half height, with the map behind it. You pull it up to cover more of the map.

Built as ADR-0053 D8, in three briefs: guide-dossier-panel (frames 1 and 3: the dossier over the map, or as a sheet), guide-trip-lens (the strip and badges, read-only, Dining and Hotels), and guide-slot-filling (Add to Trip, slot focus, Stay here, drag onto a cell). The hatched "Travel day" cell is illustrative only: Galavant has no travel-day fact. Distance to the night's hotel ships as straight-line distance, not a travel time. Candidate hotels are ADR-0053 OQ5.
