# Denmark — founding conversation

**Shape:** six rural nights, then six nights in Copenhagen. Rural: **South Funen (Falsled Kro) 3 nights → southern island route by ferry → Møn (Ellevilde) 3 nights → drive into Copenhagen.**

## What we are optimizing for

- Rural Denmark that complements Copenhagen rather than duplicating it.
- Countryside and coast with 2–4 mile walks, not all-day hikes.
- Small, personal hotels with a sense of place over large corporate luxury.
- Food matters, but flavor-first and warm hospitality over long concept-heavy tasting menus.
- Easy transfer days; four- to five-hour transfers are a real negative.
- A base should justify its nights without leaning on one flagship dinner.

## Why 3 + 3 instead of 2 + 2 + 2

Three two-night stops mean more packing, two internal transfer days, one full stationary day per base, and pressure to eat the flagship dinner everywhere. Three nights each gives four full days without a hotel move, more weather flexibility, and time to inhabit the places. No third southern stop is strong enough to break that.

## The transfer

Falsled → Svendborg → Tåsinge → Langeland → Spodsbjerg–Tårs ferry → Lolland/Falster → Møn. Avoids the motorway backtrack over the Great Belt; the 45-minute ferry becomes part of the day. Book a departure, don't over-engineer the day.

## The northern trip, later

Skagen, Henne and Mols are not rejected; they belong to a trip where the north is the point: Germany → Henne → Skagen → Gothenburg.

## Recheck before booking

- Restaurant opening days for the travel dates (Falsled, Norðdisk, Ellevilde, ND122, Egn).
- Best room categories at Falsled and Ellevilde.
- Whether a Dragsholm lunch fits the route without a silly detour; if not, skip it.
- First reviews of Henne under Alan Bates.
- Ferry timing and booking.
- The Copenhagen hotel and dining plan.
